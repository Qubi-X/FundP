Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum to start?

Account management enquiries begin from $150 of funded capital. Larger balances allow more flexible position sizing.

Do you guarantee profits?

No. CFD trading is high risk and losses are possible, including loss of your entire deposit. Nobody can honestly guarantee returns, and our motto is an aim, not a promise.

Do I keep control of my money?

Yes. Funds are held in your own Deriv account. Deposits and withdrawals are made by you, and the scope of any trading authority is agreed in writing.

How will I see what is happening in my account?

You can view activity directly on Deriv, and you receive regular reports on trades, results and risk.

How are fees structured?

Fee terms are explained and agreed in writing before you fund an account. Ask when you enquire.

Is this regulated or licensed?

Deriv is the broker and holds its own licences. Account management rules differ by country, so confirm what applies where you live. Current regulatory details are available on request.

Who can use this service?

Adults who understand the risks and can afford to lose the capital they commit. Never trade with money you need for essentials.
